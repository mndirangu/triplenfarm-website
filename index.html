A farm built to connect

More than separate enterprises.

Triple N is best described as a women-owned and women-led integrated climate-smart farm using circular agriculture principles. We reuse farm resources where practical, reduce avoidable waste, improve water efficiency and diversify production—without making misleading claims that the farm is fully organic or completely waste-free.

Interactive farm system

See how the enterprises support one another.

The farm is not presented as a perfect closed loop. Instead, it uses practical circular principles: reusing residues where safe, producing some feed inputs on-farm, composting manure, and using solar energy to support irrigation.

Transparent sustainability: Triple N is not currently a fully organic farm. Approved commercial inputs may be used responsibly when needed.

Women growing enterprise

A practical farm with a bigger ambition.

Triple N is building a commercially viable farm that also demonstrates how small and growing agricultural enterprises can diversify, use renewable energy and make more productive use of farm resources.

The farm is rooted in food production and customer service, while creating space for farmer learning, youth engagement, women’s enterprise and climate-smart innovation.
